Current Practice Times

We normally practice every Monday and Thursday from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM at Wasserland on the BTHV hockey pitch. As our practice times and locations may change from time to time, please contact us on Instagram or by email before attending your first session.

New players are always welcome - just bring sportswear for outdoor practice. We look forward to meeting you!
